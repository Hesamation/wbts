# FactSettle demo attribution

FactSettle is MIT licensed; see LICENSE in this folder.

The keyed Merkle fact-proof format and the three original constructed insurance
samples come from InferPay by Taejee Kim and contributors:
https://github.com/taejeekim/InferPay/tree/c7f773f144ff01092348185ed657dfe729fcec44

The original MIT license is included as INFERPAY-LICENSE. The browser proof checker
implements the compatible fact/vacancy subset. This is not the full InferPay
application, an upstream partnership or an endorsement. The samples are test
inputs, not actual insurance terms, private datasets or customer cases.

Full source and contract/client details are in https://github.com/Hesamation/wbts
(currently a private repository).
