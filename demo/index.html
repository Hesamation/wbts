    WHY THIS CONTRACT EXISTS

    Checking a proof doesn't
    make the buyer pay.

    With “deliver, then verify, then transfer,” an honest specialist still depends on the buyer sending payment. Here the buyer escrows the price first. After the specialist accepts the terms, a valid proof directly credits that fact's share.

    NEAR authenticates each participant, holds the escrow, checks proofs and allocates credit atomically, then handles withdrawals through transfer callbacks. No separate buyer approval is needed for a valid delivery.

    This is a focused implementation of proof-verifying escrow. It checks consistency with a buyer-chosen dataset, not external truth or general AI reasoning.
